FAQ

Questions clients ask us

Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll help right away.

Which areas of Oman do you cover?

Our head office is in Azaiba, Muscat, and we carry out projects across the Sultanate — including Ash Sharqiyah North, where we have worked in Ibra. Contact us with your location and we'll confirm.

Do you charge for a site visit or quotation?

No. We visit your site, assess the scope and send a detailed quotation free of charge.

Can you supply materials only, without installation?

Yes. Our trading division supplies pipes, fittings and construction materials to contractors and individuals, with delivery to site.

Do you take small jobs as well as large projects?

Yes — from plumbing repairs and renovations to full building and infrastructure projects.

Are you a registered company?

Yes. Maulam Safnan Trading & Co. L.L.C. is a Limited Liability Company registered in the Sultanate of Oman, Commercial Registration No. 1030567.
