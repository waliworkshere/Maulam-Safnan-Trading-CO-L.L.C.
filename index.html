About the company

Trading and contracting, built on Omani ground.

Maulam Safnan Trading & Co. L.L.C. is a commercial business entity registered in the Sultanate of Oman as a Limited Liability Company under Omani corporate regulations.

The company works in two connected fields — commercial trading and project contracting. Our project engineers and technical staff oversee works on site, while our trading arm supplies the materials projects depend on.

Our head office is in Azaiba, Muscat, and our project presence has included Ibra (Sifalat Ibra) in the Ash Sharqiyah North Governorate.
